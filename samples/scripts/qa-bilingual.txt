Matthew: Thank you for listening. We have a few minutes left, so let me open the floor for questions. Please go ahead.
Takumi: すみません、質問です。ライブ配信の遅延は、どのくらいまで短くできますか。
Matthew: Good question. With short segments and a tuned player, we reach about three seconds from the camera to the screen. With WebRTC, you can go below one second, but it costs more and it scales less easily.
Kazuha: ありがとうございます。複数のCDNを使う場合、費用はどのように管理していますか。
Matthew: We set a budget per event, and the player prefers the cheapest provider that meets the quality target. If that provider slows down, traffic moves to the next one automatically.
Tomoko: モバイル回線が弱い会場でも、安定して視聴できますか。
Matthew: Yes, in most cases. The bitrate ladder includes a very low bitrate rendition, and the player drops to it quickly when the throughput falls. Viewers see a lower resolution, but the stream does not stop.
Takumi: 最後に、日本語の字幕には対応していますか。
Matthew: Subtitles are on our roadmap. For now, you can use a separate translation service, and we provide the audio and the timing information for it. Thank you for all your questions, and thank you again for coming.
